Scoring & Decision Cheat Sheet

This reference explains how selections are scored. Issue Impact and Change Risk are calculated independently and combined only after the assessment is submitted. Confirmed Safety or Regulatory/Compliance impact bypasses scoring entirely and returns ESCALATE directly — it does not add points to Issue Impact. Issue Impact uses weighted scoring so severity and customer impact carry more influence than lower-order factors such as occurrence or workaround.
